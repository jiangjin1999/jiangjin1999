## Hi, I'm Jin Jiang (姜金) 👋

You can call me **Justin**. I'm a Ph.D. student at **Peking University**.

My earlier work focused on **LLM reasoning**, and my current focus is **multimodal agentic coding**: using visual feedback and orchestrating models as tools to expand what coding agents can do.

I'm also interested in **AI for Health**, especially biological aging and lifelong digital twins. I enjoy digitizing and documenting my life, particularly health information.

I maintain [Awesome Multimodal Agentic Coding](https://github.com/jiangjin1999/awesome-multimodal-agentic-coding), a collection of papers, benchmarks, demos, and tools.
